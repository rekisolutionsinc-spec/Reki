What is Elevate Hub?

Elevate Hub is Spursbase Foundation's transformative learning and skills development centre. We exist to bridge the gap between education and employability, providing young people with access to quality learning, TVET (Technical and Vocational Education and Training), and personal development opportunities.

Our training approach combines academic enrichment, creativity, innovation, and life skills to prepare students and young people for a productive and purposeful future. Elevate Hub serves as a community resource, open to vulnerable children seeking skills, volunteers seeking structured engagement, and young people ready to elevate their potential.

The Challenge

Across Ghana's underserved communities, the gap between education and employment is wide. Children complete school without marketable skills; young people graduate without pathways to work. Without access to skills training, talent goes untapped and potential remains locked.

Education Without Employability

Many children in marginalised communities complete their basic education without acquiring practical skills that enable economic independence. The result is a generation ready to learn but without the tools to build productive futures.

Limited Access to TVET

Technical and Vocational Education and Training remains inaccessible for many young people from low-income backgrounds, either too far away, too expensive, or too disconnected from community realities.

Volunteers Without Structure

Many passionate individuals want to contribute to community development but lack structured opportunities. Elevate Hub creates pathways for volunteers to contribute meaningfully while also growing their own skills and experience.

Our Impact

650 Girls Reached Through Digital Skills

Girls in ICT Day at Awudome SHS engaged 650 girls with hands-on digital skills exposure and career awareness, demonstrating what Elevate Hub's skills training model can achieve at scale.

70 Students, Rural Innovation Challenge

The Rural Community Innovation Challenge at Mafi Wudzrolo engaged 70 students in hands-on problem-solving, building creativity, teamwork, and entrepreneurship mindsets that form the foundation of Elevate Hub's approach.

110% Growth in One Year

Spursbase Foundation achieved 110% growth in 2025, driven in part by expanding skills and volunteer programming that amplified the reach and impact of every other programme area.

Volunteer Network Growing

Our structured volunteer programme is building a growing community of purpose-driven individuals committed to creating opportunity, restoring dignity, and contributing to Ghana's next generation of leaders.

Volunteer With Us

Do you want to make a meaningful difference in your community and beyond? Spursbase Foundation invites passionate, purpose-driven individuals to join our Volunteer Program and be part of lasting change.

What You'll Do

Support grassroots education and child advocacy initiatives. Gain hands-on experience in community engagement, social impact, and life skills empowerment. Participate in educational outreach, health and wellbeing activities, mentorship sessions, and community events.

Commitment

Whether you are available for four weeks or up to one year, your time, skills, and commitment can help empower children, strengthen communities, and advance equitable access to education. Volunteers may join on-site or remotely, depending on availability, skills, and programme needs.

How to Apply

Send your CV and a motivation letter to: volunteer@spursbasefoundation.org. A volunteer contribution of €300 applies to support programme coordination, training, and logistics (terms and conditions apply).
