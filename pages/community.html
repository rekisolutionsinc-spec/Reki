Our Objective

Many communities in underserved areas find it difficult to understand how to effectively support their children's development. Spursbase Foundation believes that training parents and caregivers to play an active role in their children's growth is fundamental to breaking cycles of poverty and ensuring children remain in school.

Our Community Development Programme works directly with families, equipping parents, guardians, and caregivers with the skills, knowledge, and financial tools to create stable environments where education remains a priority.

The Challenge

For many families in rural Ghana, keeping a child in school is not a question of values but of affordability. Financial pressure at the household level often forces children out of school, reinforcing cycles of poverty and limiting opportunity for generations.

Poverty as a Barrier

Many families in marginalised communities lack the financial stability to cover basic education costs, uniforms, shoes, exercise books, leading children to miss school or drop out entirely not because they lack ambition but because circumstances leave no other option.

Young Parents

In the communities we serve, some children become parents while still in school. Without support, many young mothers are forced to drop out entirely. Empowering caregivers, including young parents, helps keep education within reach for both parent and child.

Clothing Poverty

Many children attend school in distress or miss classes entirely due to insufficient clothing, worn-out footwear, or the absence of suitable items for daily living. Education does not begin at the school gate, it begins with dignity at home.

Our Impact

500+ Students & Families

Supported through the Nunana Project with home wear, school shoes, and essential clothing across the Volta, North East, and Central Regions, reducing absenteeism linked to lack of clothing.

157 Children

Participated in the Christmas of Hope celebration at Mafi Seva, experiencing joy, nutritious meals, storytelling, creative activities, and clothing distributions in a safe, festive environment.

Improved School Retention

Through the Saving the Future programme, children supported by family empowerment initiatives show significantly improved school attendance and retention rates, with parents more actively involved in education decisions.

Strengthened Community Trust

Our community-first approach has deepened relationships with local families, volunteers, and community leaders, building a network of care that extends well beyond individual programme activities.

Our Approach

We believe sustainable change comes from within communities. Our Community Development Programme works alongside families rather than for them, building local ownership and ensuring every intervention creates lasting roots.

Parent-Centred Programming

We engage parents and caregivers directly, recognising that a child's success at school depends as much on what happens at home as what happens in the classroom.

Dignity-First Distribution

Every clothing and resource distribution is carried out with respect and care, ensuring beneficiaries feel seen, valued, and supported rather than stigmatised.

Long-Term Family Empowerment

Through the Miatia Project and Saving the Future, we help families build financial stability over time, moving beyond one-off support toward sustainable livelihoods that keep children in school for the long term.
