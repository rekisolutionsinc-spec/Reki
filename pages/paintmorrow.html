Paint Tomorrow Fundraising Event

The Spursbase Foundation is proud to present its Paint Tomorrow fundraising event, scheduled for September 20, 2025, aimed at raising GHS 50,000 to support education, health, and empowerment programs in rural Ghana.

This pivotal event will be held at C3 Ignite Church, East Legon, La Bawaleshie, bringing together over 200 stakeholders including community members, corporate partners, volunteers, and supporters dedicated to transforming the lives of children in underserved rural communities.

About Paint Tomorrow

Rural communities in Ghana face significant educational and health challenges that hinder the development and well-being of children and their families. Many schools lack adequate infrastructure, teaching materials, and qualified teachers, which leads to poor academic performance and high dropouts, especially among girls. Health barriers, including limited access to healthcare, poor nutrition, and preventable diseases, further reduce children's ability to participate fully in school and contribute to the cycle of poverty.

Since 2019, Spursbase Foundation has positively impacted over 1,000 children and 500 families through comprehensive programs enhancing education, health services, and economic independence. The proceeds from Paint Tomorrow will directly support providing school supplies to 700 children, facilitating health screenings for 300 children, and empowering parents through business development initiatives in three under-resourced villages.

Key Objectives of Paint Tomorrow

  • Raise 50,000 GHS to support education, health, and empowerment programs in rural Ghana.
  • Provide school supplies to 200 children improving access to basic education.
  • Facilitate health screenings for 300 children, addressing preventable diseases and promoting early diagnosis.
  • Support parents with business development opportunities to enable sustainable income, reducing child labor or school dropout due to poverty.
  • Engage and mobilize at least 200 stakeholders, including corporate donors, artists, volunteers, and community leaders.
  • Increase public awareness of the interconnected challenges of education and health in rural areas through creative engagement.

Main Activities

  • Speeches delivering an overview of Spursbase Foundation’s mission and progress by the founder and board members.
  • Interactive panel discussion focusing on challenges and solutions in rural education and health.
  • Music performances and food providing a culturally rich atmosphere for community celebration.
  • Storytelling sessions featuring real-life stories from beneficiaries, volunteers, and children illustrating the program’s impact.

Expected Outcomes

  1. 200 children receive essential educational supplies, improving school attendance and participation.
  2. Health screenings for 300 children enable early detection and treatment, promoting better health and reduced absenteeism.
  3. At least 50 parents empowered through business support programs, boosting household incomes and resilience.
  4. Raised funds of 50,000 GHS to sustain Spursbase Foundation’s rural programs.
  5. Heightened awareness among 200+ participants and stakeholders, encouraging advocacy and long-term partnerships.
  6. Strengthened community pride through active participation via art, storytelling, and cultural expression.
  7. Enhanced visibility of Spursbase Foundation as a key leader addressing rural development sustainably and creatively.

Conclusion

The Paint Tomorrow fundraising event represents a unique opportunity to catalyze transformative change for children and families in rural Ghana. Through community participation, partnerships, and strategic funding, this initiative aims to break the cycles of poverty and inequality, ensuring every child, regardless of birthplace, can learn, grow, and thrive.

Together, we will paint a brighter future for rural communities across Ghana.
