Bridging Education and Employability

Across Ghana, thousands of young people graduate each year with degrees, diplomas, and ambition, but without clear pathways into employment, leadership, or meaningful contribution. The Elevate Graduate Learning Program exists to change that.

Hosted within Spursbase Foundation's Elevate Hub, our Graduate Program provides structured mentorship, skills enrichment, professional development, and hands-on community experience, building a generation of purpose-driven, employable young leaders.

Programme Pillars
What the Program Offers

Professional Mentorship

Matched with experienced mentors from education, NGO, tech, health, and business sectors who guide your career development.

Skills Development

Practical workshops covering project management, communication, digital tools, leadership, and community development.

Community Engagement

Hands-on experience in Spursbase Foundation's programmes, gaining real impact, portfolio evidence, and sector knowledge.

Professional Network

Access to a growing network of partners, alumni, and sector leaders across Ghana and internationally.

Your Journey
How the Program Works

Apply & Be Assessed

Submit your application and motivation. Shortlisted candidates receive a brief virtual interview to assess alignment and commitment.

Onboarding & Goal Setting

Structured onboarding session, setting personal development goals, meeting your mentor, and understanding expectations.

Learning & Engagement

Skills workshops, mentorship sessions, and active contribution to Spursbase Foundation's community programmes.

Capstone Project

Each participant develops and presents a community-focused project demonstrating skills and real-world impact.

Graduation & Alumni Network

Receive a Spursbase Foundation certificate and join our growing alumni network, staying connected to opportunities and the Foundation's mission.
