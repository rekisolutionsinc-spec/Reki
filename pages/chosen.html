The Challenge

  • Barriers to education due to poverty
  • Limited access to health and nutrition
  • Risk of child labor and exploitation
  • Cycles of poverty without intervention

How Miatia Works

  • Monthly or annual godparent commitment
  • Personal connection through updates
  • Education, health, and mentorship support
  • Community-based long-term impact

Our Impact Areas

Physical Needs Support

Provide food, clothing, shelter, and hygiene essentials to meet children's daily needs.

Education and Development

Help children attend school, get learning materials, and receive tutoring for long-term success.

Health & Life Skills Support

Access to healthcare services, mental wellness programs, and skills for personal growth.

Family Support

Reintegrating children into supportive family environments and strengthening community ties.
