About the Run
Five Years. One Movement. Your Step.

Run for Change is the flagship advocacy campaign of Spursbase Foundation. What began as a small community effort has grown into a national movement, amplifying the voices of children affected by poor infrastructure, teacher shortages, and educational exclusion across Ghana.

The 2026 edition marks our 5th anniversary. Rather than a ceremony, we chose action. Every participant who laces up on 26th September is making a statement: that children in underserved communities deserve to be seen, heard, and supported.

This is your invitation to be part of something that matters.

The Route
A Walk Through the Heart of Accra

The Run for Change Advocacy Walk moves through some of Accra's most visible thoroughfares, designed to maximise public engagement and put the demand for education equity directly in front of thousands of citizens.

Start, Shiashie, Accra

Assembly at 7:30 AM. Opening ceremony, addresses, and official send-off at 8:00 AM sharp.

Through the Urban Corridor

A high-visibility route engaging thousands of citizens, sparking conversation about education equity in Ghana.

Finish, University of Ghana, Legon

Medal presentation, souvenir collection, post-run celebration, closing ceremony, and photos.
