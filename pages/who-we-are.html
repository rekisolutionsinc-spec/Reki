Who we are

Working alongside communities to open pathways for children.

Spursbase Foundation is a Ghanaian nonprofit working with children, families, schools and communities, particularly in underserved rural areas. We focus on expanding educational opportunity, supporting child wellbeing and helping young people build skills for the future.

Our purpose

Every child should have the opportunity to learn, thrive and reach their potential, regardless of where they are born.

We work with children, families, schools and communities to expand access to education, strengthen learning and protection, and create pathways to skills and opportunity.

Why our work is connected

A child’s experience of education is shaped by more than the classroom. Learning, safety, health, family circumstances and future opportunities are connected. We therefore combine direct support for children with work alongside the people and institutions around them.

How we work

  • Community-led: We listen to children, families, schools and local partners, and shape our work around the realities they identify.
  • Inclusive: We focus on children and young people facing barriers to learning, participation and opportunity.
  • Safe: We treat safeguarding, dignity and wellbeing as part of every programme.
  • Learning-focused: We use experience and evidence to improve our programmes and make responsible choices.
  • Sustainable: We build partnerships and local capacity so progress can continue beyond individual activities.

Our story

Spursbase Foundation grew from a commitment to children whose opportunities are limited by where they live and the circumstances around them. Our experience in communities has taught us to move beyond isolated interventions and build more connected pathways through learning, protection, family support and skills.
