About Paint Tomorrow

The Paint Tomorrow event is a fundraising effort targeting GHS 50,000 to provide school supplies, health screenings, and business support to rural Ghanaian communities. Through powerful collaboration, cultural events, and generosity, we aim to transform education and health outcomes for children and families.

Key Objectives

  • Raise 50,000 GHS for education, health, and empowerment programs.
  • Provide school supplies to 700 children.
  • Facilitate health screenings for 300 children to detect illnesses early.
  • Empower parents through business development opportunities.
  • Engage over 200 stakeholders in mobilizing rural development.
