Become a Volunteer

Your time and skills are powerful tools for change. Join the Spursbase Foundation volunteer community and help us reach more children in underserved communities across Ghana and beyond.

Make a Meaningful Difference

Spursbase Foundation invites passionate, purpose-driven individuals to join our Volunteer Program and be part of lasting change. Whether you are a student, a professional, or simply someone who cares, your commitment can help empower children, strengthen communities, and advance equitable access to education.

Volunteers may join us on-site in Ghana or remotely, depending on availability, skills, and programme needs. We welcome volunteers from Ghana and across the world.

Contribution: A volunteer contribution of €300 applies to cover coordination, training, and logistics. International volunteers can complete payment online during registration.

Your Role
What You'll Do

Education Outreach

Support reading programmes, digital classrooms, teacher training, and student mentorship in underserved communities.

Health & Wellbeing

Assist with health awareness campaigns, menstrual health education, and wellbeing activities that keep children healthy and in school.

Community Events

Help organise innovation challenges, skills days, and community celebrations like Christmas of Hope.

Digital & Media Support

Use your digital, content, design, or social media skills to amplify Spursbase Foundation's mission and reach.

Skills & Training

Deliver workshops at Elevate Hub, sharing professional skills with young people working toward employment and independence.

Advocacy & Awareness

Champion the rights of marginalised children through campaigns, virtual events, and community dialogue.

Commitment & Details

Duration

Volunteer commitments range from 4 weeks to 12 months. Whether you have a few weeks or a full year to give, we have a meaningful role for you.

On-Site or Remote

Volunteers can join us on the ground in Ghana or contribute remotely from anywhere in the world, depending on the programme and your availability.

Who Can Apply

We welcome students, graduates, professionals, and retirees who are passionate about education, child welfare, and community development.

What Happens After You Apply

Once you submit your application you will receive a confirmation email with your next steps, including information on orientation and onboarding before you begin.
