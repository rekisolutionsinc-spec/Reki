Our Objective

Spursbase Foundation's Education Programme supports the hardest-to-reach out-of-school children around the world facing barriers to education including poverty, discrimination, conflict, challenging geographies, and climate change.

We and our partners scale up existing education projects in support of the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goal of achieving quality education for all by 2030.

Our Work

Spursbase Foundation's Education Programme is built around a uniquely flexible model. Our local and national partners tailor their grassroots solutions to the needs of out-of-school children in their communities and countries, in line with predefined goals and success metrics.

This allows us to bridge formal with non-formal education approaches, reaching and engaging children no matter their geography, circumstances, or context. Our approach is guided by 10 core principles:

  1. Adopt a rights-based approach to educationProtect children's universal right to learn by working with partners to overcome barriers that prevent them from accessing quality primary education.
  2. Identify communities with the greatest needFocus resources on areas with a substantial number of out-of-school children and partners with a proven track record.
  3. Commit to evidence-based programmingSupport informed decision-making with sound educational data that highlights national, regional and local disparities.
  4. Work within national education plansAlign project activities with a country's national education plans, ensuring accountability at every level of governance.
  5. Emphasise lifelong learningContribute to each individual's ability and skills development beyond academia, empowering them to make informed choices.
  6. Harness the power of partnershipsRecognise the importance of joint programming for reaching the hardest-to-reach children through innovative approaches.
  7. Promote value creation through partnershipsCatalyse quality education opportunities by leveraging strong partnerships with organisations that have successful, tested models.
  8. Ensure programming sustainabilityHelp mobilise long-term funding sources through the promotion, replication, and expansion of strategies proven to reach children.
  9. Learn from monitoring and evaluationCollect, analyse and use data on opportunities and obstacles that arise as children access and complete their education.
  10. Advocate for out-of-school childrenCommit to building a global alliance to advocate for the right of every child to be enrolled in primary education.

Way Forward

In addition to maintaining support for existing education interventions, Spursbase Foundation has committed to reaching an additional 50,000 children each year. Our forward-looking At-Risk Initiative helps prevent vulnerable children from dropping out of education.

At-Risk Initiative

Identifying and supporting children at the highest risk of dropping out before they fall through the cracks, through early warning systems, community engagement, and targeted support.

Digital Learning Expansion

Rolling out low-cost digital tools to remote classrooms, giving teachers and students access to quality learning materials regardless of location.

Girls' Education Pledge

Doubling the number of girls supported through scholarships, mentorship, and community engagement programmes by 2027.

Our Reach

Spursbase Foundation's education work spans communities across Ghana and extends to partner organisations in sub-Saharan Africa. We currently operate in 47 schools across 6 regions of Ghana.

Ghana

Our primary area of operation. We work with 47 schools across the Greater Accra, Central, Northern, Volta, Eastern, and Western regions, supporting over 12,400 children directly.

Sub-Saharan Africa

Through partnerships with local organisations we extend our reach to communities in West and East Africa, sharing resources, models, and best practices.

Global Network

We participate in global education coalitions and advocate for the right to education in international forums, amplifying the voices of the communities we serve.

Challenge

In spite of the progress Ghana has made in improving access to education, thousands of children are still prevented from going to school and learning. Classes are overcrowded, water and sanitation facilities are inadequate, and trained teachers and materials are in short supply.

Early Learning Gap

Nearly 623,500 children of primary school age are still not enrolled. One in four children in the kindergarten age range are not in pre-school, leaving a critical gap in their foundational development.

Adolescent Girls

Although Ghana has closed the gender gap at primary level, it remains high at secondary level. Adolescent girls are often unable to complete education due to poverty, gender inequality, and long distances from school.

Inclusive Education

Children with disabilities often lose out because they are invisible in data and overlooked during programme implementation. Without disability-friendly infrastructure, they face irregular attendance and eventually drop out.

Solution

Spursbase Foundation is working with the Ministry of Education, Ghana Education Service, and other partners to create a more child-friendly environment in schools so that all children can learn.

Early Learning Centres

Establishing and equipping community-based pre-school centres so that every child enters primary school ready to learn.

Girls' Education Support

Scholarships, mentorship, safe transport, and school materials targeted specifically at keeping adolescent girls enrolled and learning.

Disability-Friendly Schools

Retrofitting and designing school environments that are fully accessible and welcoming for children with physical disabilities and learning differences.

Teacher Development

Ongoing professional training, mentoring, and resource provision for teachers to equip them to support every child in their classroom.
